Questions

Before you decide

What exactly do I get?

A 25-page A4 PDF: a welcome, 18 practical chapters, three fill-in worksheets and a 30-day challenge, plus a closing message.

Can I use it outside the United States?

Yes. Examples use dollars, but the methods work in any currency. A few mentions of regulators are examples, so check the rules that apply where you live.

Does it tell me what to invest in?

No. It focuses on budgeting, saving, emergency funds, debt and habits. It warns against get-rich-quick schemes and does not promote any investment.

Can I print the worksheets?

Yes. The budget template, spending tracker and savings tracker are designed to be printed or copied into a notebook.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is general education. Rules, fees, interest rates and taxes differ by country and change over time, so speak to a registered adviser about your own situation.
